A Note on the Collection

C:\WEINGARD.ONLINE> TYPE METHODOLOGY.TXT

> METHODOLOGY & THE STATE OF THE COLLECTOR

This was not a scientific process in a lab. It was years of torture and harassment, documented by someone who was simultaneously physically ill (referred to hospice at one point for an MRSA infection), mentally ill (Baker Acted during this period), and being emotionally and sexually abused by a romantic partner.

The collection process was not perfect, and neither was the mental state of the collector. But every attempt has been made to be faithful: messages are preserved uncut and unedited; every row cites its original source file so it can be checked against the export it came from; duplicates were removed only on exact matches (never on “looks similar”); timestamps missing from one export but present on an identical line elsewhere were recovered and the row flagged undated where they could not be; and where identity is uncertain, a record sits in a suspected bucket rather than a confirmed one. Nothing here was added, softened, or prettified. The goal is not perfection — it is that every single line can be corroborated against the original.
